Bridging the Gap Between Physics and Capital.

⚡ The Hardware Funding Bottleneck

Software iterates at the speed of thought. Hardware is bottlenecked by physical reality and capital. While founders understand the physics of their designs, Venture Capitalists require extensive Technical Due Diligence to verify unit economics, CapEx, and physical viability.

This friction delays innovation and increases the barrier to entry for deep-tech startups. CADfactory eliminates that gap.

🌐 Dynamic Supply Chain Intelligence

CADfactory is an enterprise-grade, AI-driven physics-to-capital compiler. By analyzing raw geometric constraints directly from CAD files, we generate mathematically verified venture assets instantly.

To mitigate supply chain risks, CADfactory acts as a dynamic routing engine. Rather than relying on rigid manufacturing bottlenecks, our platform identifies optimal global or nearshore hubs based on real-time cost, lead time, and material requirements.
